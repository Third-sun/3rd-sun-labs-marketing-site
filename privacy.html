SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This privacy policy describes how mobile phone numbers and related information are collected, used, and protected in connection with the 3rd Sun Labs Operations Alerts SMS program (the “Program”). The Program delivers automated operational alerts and system status notifications from the 3rd Sun Labs automation platform to its administrator.

Information we collect

When you opt in to the Program, we collect the mobile phone number you provide, your consent selection, and the date and time of your opt-in. We also retain a log of messages sent for operational and troubleshooting purposes.

How your information is used

Your mobile phone number is used solely to deliver the automated operational alert messages you have requested, such as data pipeline job status, error alerts, and system health notifications. It is not used for advertising or promotional messaging.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use-case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with, or sold to, any third parties.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on system events and application activity.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan. Contact your wireless provider for details about your plan.

Opting out and getting help

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, you will receive a final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again. For assistance, reply HELP to any message or contact us using the information below.

Data retention and security

Phone numbers and consent records are retained only as long as needed to operate the Program and to meet compliance obligations, and are protected using industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued participation in the Program after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the Program can be sent to support@3rdsunlabs.com.
