web style guide.

A practical visual system derived from the selected logo. This guide is designed for the website, presentations, social graphics, and digital marketing.

01 / Logo

Primary identity

The connected three-node emblem communicates intelligent systems, coordinated automation, and the “third sun” concept. Use the full lockup for formal brand moments and the icon alone for navigation, favicons, app tiles, and social avatars.

Preferred use

Place the gradient logo on navy or similarly dark backgrounds with generous clear space.

Minimum clear space

Keep at least one central-node diameter around all sides of the icon or lockup.

Do not

Stretch, rotate, recolor individual parts, add heavy shadows, or place the white wordmark on a light background.

Production note

The supplied identity began as raster artwork. A professionally traced vector master is recommended before large-format print work.

03 / Typography

Modern without feeling like science fiction.

Use Space Grotesk for headlines, navigation, and short emphasis. Use Inter for body copy, forms, labels, and dense information. Both include broad fallback stacks in the CSS.

Space Grotesk

Bold headlines, compact spacing, strong geometric character.

Inter

Clear body typography for detailed explanations, service descriptions, and calls to action.

04 / Voice

Confident, practical, outcome-focused.

Lead with the result

Describe the operational benefit before discussing the underlying technology.

Make complexity understandable

Use precise language, but translate jargon when the audience does not need it.

Avoid hype

Prefer “reduce manual processing” over vague claims such as “revolutionize everything.”

Use the tagline deliberately

“Innovation. Automation. Impact.” works best as a signature line, not repeated in every section.
